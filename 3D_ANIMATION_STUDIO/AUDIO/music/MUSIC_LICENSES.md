## Using external music in the future
Allowed sources: YouTube Audio Library (check the per‑track "attribution required" flag), CC0 tracks, and CC‑BY tracks (credit in the description). Add a row here **before** a track enters a mix. Never use commercial songs, "royalty‑free" tracks without a written licence, or AI‑music services whose terms forbid monetisation or claim ownership.

## Content‑ID note
Original synthesised music cannot match existing Content‑ID references, so it is the lowest‑risk option for monetised Shorts.
