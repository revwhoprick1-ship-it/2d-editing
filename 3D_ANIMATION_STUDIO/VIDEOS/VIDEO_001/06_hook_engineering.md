# VIDEO_001 — Hook Engineering

The opening has three jobs: (1) make the premise clear, (2) create a reason to be curious, and (3) set up a question that the video will answer.

## Candidates (first line + first visual)

| ID | First line | First visual |
|---|---|---|
| H1 | "You've been shrunk smaller than a blood cell… and your own body is about to attack you." | Eyes snap open, then a pull‑back reveals him riding a blood cell through a rushing tunnel |
| H2 | "This red cushion is one of your blood cells. And you're riding it." | Close on a hand patting a red surface, then a reveal |
| H3 | "Your heart is about to launch you at full speed." | Mascot gripping a disc while a dark chamber looms |
| H4 | "What if you were shrunk and lived inside your own body?" | Title‑style shot, mascot shrinking |
| H5 | "Nobody warned you that your own blood would try to eat you." | Pale shape looming over the mascot |

## Scores (1–5)

| Criterion | H1 | H2 | H3 | H4 | H5 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Immediate clarity | 5 | 4 | 2 | 5 | 3 |
| Curiosity (specific gap) | 5 | 3 | 4 | 3 | 5 |
| Novelty | 4 | 4 | 3 | 1 | 4 |
| Emotional impact | 4 | 2 | 4 | 2 | 4 |
| Visual potential | 4 | 5 | 4 | 3 | 3 |
| Narrative relevance | 5 | 4 | 4 | 5 | 3 (blood doesn't "eat"; a cell in it does) |
| **Total** | **27** | 22 | 21 | 19 | 22 |

## Selected: H1, with H2's visual folded in
* **Why it works.** One sentence gives a clear premise ("shrunk… inside your body") and a specific, ironic threat ("your own body… attack you"). That is an information gap of the kind Loewenstein (1994) describes: the viewer knows exactly what they don't know (*how* and *why* the body will attack). The second person draws the viewer into the story (narrative transportation, Green & Brock 2000).
* **Visual match.** Frame 1 is the mascot's face, so the character is introduced immediately. The pull‑back to the blood‑cell tunnel lands on the words "smaller than a blood cell". A pale cell gliding past in the background on "attack you" gives the eye something to track and quietly sets up the predator.
* **What it avoids:** no logo, no "today we'll explore", no slow title card.
* **No retention numbers are claimed.** The choice rests on the principles above and on the comparison table, not on invented statistics.
