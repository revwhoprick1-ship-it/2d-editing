# VIDEO_001 — Script Draft v1 (first draft, kept unedited for the record)

> 1. Imagine you were shrunk down smaller than a cell and dropped inside your own body.
> 2. You'd land in your bloodstream, surrounded by red blood cells — each one about seven to eight micrometers wide.
> 3. The blood carries you to your heart, which pumps more than five liters every minute.
> 4. Then on to your lungs, where oxygen enters your blood through a membrane just half a micrometer thick.
> 5. But your immune system notices something foreign — you. A neutrophil, the most common white blood cell, attacks.
> 6. It tries to engulf you, but you manage to escape back into the heart.
> 7. Your heart pumps you out into the rest of your body.
> 8. The human body is amazing. Follow for more!

About 105 words, about 40 s.
