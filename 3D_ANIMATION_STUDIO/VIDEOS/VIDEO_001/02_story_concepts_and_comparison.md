# VIDEO_001 — Three Story Concepts & Comparison

## Concept A — "The Hitchhiker" (circulation tour)
* **Hook:** "This red disc is one of your blood cells, and you're riding it."
* **Conflict:** Environmental only: currents, turbulence, the heart's force.
* **Discoveries:** Vein → right heart → lungs (gas exchange) → left heart → aorta.
* **Escalation:** The current gets faster and the heart louder, ending in a launch into the aorta.
* **Payoff:** "Your heart does this all day, every day."

## Concept B — "Intruder" (your own immune system hunts you)
* **Hook:** "You've been shrunk smaller than a blood cell… and your own body is about to attack you."
* **Conflict:** A neutrophil treats the mascot as foreign material and tries to engulf him.
* **Discoveries:** RBC scale, the heartbeat as a threat, valves (lub‑dub), lung capillaries and gas exchange, the neutrophil.
* **Escalation:** Ambient danger (heart), then a narrow tunnel, then a predator in the same tunnel, then capture.
* **Twist:** It's *his own* body, and it doesn't recognise him. He escapes but loses a shoe.
* **Payoff:** The left ventricle launches him into the aorta. Last line: a white blood cell is still trying to digest his sneaker.

## Concept C — "Every Breath Is a Hurricane" (trapped in an air sac)
* **Hook:** "You're stuck inside one of your own air sacs, and you're about to be exhaled."
* **Conflict:** Breath cycles as storms. He must cling on or be blown out.
* **Discoveries:** Alveolar sacs, the respiratory membrane, O₂ crossing into blood, CO₂ coming out.
* **Escalation:** Faster breathing, then a cough.
* **Payoff:** He is coughed out. "That's literally what you breathe out."

## Comparison (1 = weak, 5 = strong)

| Criterion | A Hitchhiker | B Intruder | C Hurricane |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Strongest opening | 3: visual, but no stakes | **5**: premise and threat in one line | 4: clear danger, harder to picture |
| 2. Easiest to understand | **5** | 4 | 3: "air sac" needs explaining |
| 3. Visual opportunities | 4 | **5**: vessels, heart, lungs, a predator cell | 3: mostly one environment |
| 4. Curiosity | 3 | **5**: "why would my body attack me?" | 4 |
| 5. Escalation | 3 | **5**: environment, then tight space, then predator, then capture | 3 |
| 6. Satisfying payoff | 2: generic | **5**: launch plus a callback joke | 4 |
| 7. Retention potential | 3 | **5** | 3 |
| 8. Producible with our pipeline (4‑core CPU, no GPU) | **5**: one reusable tunnel | 4: tunnel + heart + capillary + one cell | 2: airflow and particles are heavy |
| **Total** | 28 | **38** | 26 |

## Decision
**Concept B.** It keeps Concept A's real circulation route as its "map", so the education comes for free, and adds a predator, an irony and a payoff. It is not the most complicated option. Concept C's air‑flow simulation is more complex and less readable on a phone screen. Every environment B needs can be built from one procedural *vessel tunnel* generator (vein, capillary, aorta), plus a heart‑chamber set and a neutrophil asset.
