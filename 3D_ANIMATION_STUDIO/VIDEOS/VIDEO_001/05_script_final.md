**Runtime target:** 50–60 s · **Narrator:** studio narrator (see `AUDIO/narrator/NARRATOR.md`) · **Viewpoint:** second person. The mascot is "you".

## Change log (v1 → v2 → v2.1)
* **Hook rewritten.** Premise and threat now come in the first sentence, and the "imagine" wording is gone.
* **Numbers replaced by images:** "squishy cushion", "a single drop holds millions", "a hundred times thinner than paper".
* **Heart turned into a threat and a transition:** heartbeat motif, the valve "lub‑dub" fact, "one squeeze fires you".
* **Predator set up early.** A pale shape appears in S01, and a shadow passes the capillary wall in S07. The reveal gets its own beat (S08) with the music cut.
* **Irony stated plainly:** "To it, you're not *you*… you're an intruder."
* **The mascot now acts.** He pats, grips, squeezes, kicks and loses a shoe. The cost becomes the joke.
* **Climax is factual:** the left ventricle (main systemic pump) sends blood into the aorta.
* **The ending is a callback, not a CTA.**
* **v2.1 polish:** "It doesn't know you're you" became "To it, you're not *you*…" because it reads more naturally aloud. "Biggest artery you have" is kept (the aorta). "Main pump" replaced "strongest pump" to match the verified wording ("major pumping chamber").
* **Removed:** "follow for more", the 5 L/min line, RBC micrometre numbers, and the unverified one‑minute‑circulation claim.

## Second review of v2.1 (by the critical viewer)
* *Would I stop scrolling?* Yes. "Your own body is about to attack you" is a threat aimed at me.
* *Premise understood?* Yes, within about 4 s: small person, blood, danger.
* *Saggy section?* S07 is the slowest (an explanation). It is kept to about 6 s with active visuals (particles, colour change) and the planted shadow, so the tension carries.
* *Escalation:* heart sounds (S04), violent valves (S05), tight tunnel (S06), predator (S08–S10), launch (S11).
* *Ending:* a joke payoff for viewers who noticed the shoe. Rewatch value.
* *Remaining risk:* second person over a visible character can briefly feel odd. It is the norm for the "what if you…" format and matches the title, so it stays.
