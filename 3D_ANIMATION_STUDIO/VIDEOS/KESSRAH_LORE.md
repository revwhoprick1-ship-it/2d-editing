# "Kessrah" — series lore bible (never contradict this)

## Who Kessrah is
* **Kessrah, the One Behind the Eyelids.** A demon that lives in the moment your eyes are closed. Its face is a pale oval covered
  in dozens of **closed eyelids**; when it opens them, every eye has a **red iris**. Tall, thin, black, fingers too long.
* It is **never there when you look** — only in the dark of a blink. **Every time you blink, it gets closer** (it moves only then).

## The ritual (as the viral video says)
* 3:33 AM. A **salt circle**, **three candles**, say **"Kessrah" three times**, and **don't blink until the candles go out**.
* **You're not supposed to finish it.** Saying it the third time *opens the door*. Kessrah doesn't come when called —
  **it comes when you blink.** (Theo knows this from his cousin, who did it in 2019 and "moved away".)
* When the candles go out **all at once**, it has heard you.

## Rules
1. It needs **your blink** to move. A blink is its doorway.
2. It **wears the people around you** like masks: they turn, stare, and smile the same smile (the class).
3. It speaks **in your own voice** once it is close.
4. It takes **attendance**: it says its own name as if it were a student — it is putting itself on your list.
5. Things it touches get **chalk marks** (the board: "YOU CALLED. I CAME. DON'T BLINK.").
6. The ritual can only be undone by saying the name **backwards** ("Harssek") — but that is a guess from the video comments,
   never confirmed.
