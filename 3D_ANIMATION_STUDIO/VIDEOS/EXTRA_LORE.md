# THE EXTRA PASSENGER — lore bible (VIDEO_018+). Never contradict.

- Flight 1213, overnight, over the ocean. 212 seats. The attendant's clicker keeps counting more people than seats.
- **The Extra**: something that rides night flights by slipping into the passenger count. It has no face of its own; it
  borrows a seat, and the people around it "remember" it was always there. Each time the lights go out, it can add one more.
- Rules: (1) it cannot be counted twice by the same person; (2) it has **no reflection** in the windows; (3) if you name the wrong
  one, you lose your place in the count and become the next extra (nobody remembers you).
- **Truth (for Part 2, do not reveal early):** the extra is **the girl in 23A**. Clues planted in VIDEO_018:
  her window shows no reflection; she knows the exact count; her drawing of "the one with no face" sits in HER seat.
- The man in 22C is its first victim (dead since takeoff, the reason "he's lying" is its voice, thrown into him).
- Lawrence is real: he bought his ticket at the gate (Bob didn't know). His "I've always been here" sounds wrong because the
  Extra is copying the cadence of whoever is closest; it makes the real ones look fake.
