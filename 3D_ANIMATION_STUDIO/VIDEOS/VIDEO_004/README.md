## 4. Science notes
* With gravity gone, the atmosphere stays for 10 s (gas expands far too slowly to matter), the Earth keeps spinning, and objects keep their current velocity. Nothing "falls up".
* Earth's rotation: the surface at the equator moves at ~465 m/s, and the centripetal acceleration is 0.034 m/s². In 10 s, a free body drifts ≈ 1.7 m from the curving ground (½·a·t²). Less at higher latitudes.
* People: the release of the normal force lets compressed legs/spine push you up gently; we show ~0.15 m/s. A jump at 0.7 m/s (animated) keeps going.
* Story time is slowed: the on‑screen clock counts the 10 seconds over ~20 s of video, and the falls are slowed for readability.
