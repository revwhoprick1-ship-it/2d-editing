# VIDEO_002 — Script draft v1 (unedited) and critical review

## Draft v1 (~40 s)
> 1. What if everyone on Earth farted at the exact same time?
> 2. At 3:14 PM, 8 billion people let one rip.
> 3. News anchors froze. Weddings stopped. Even the library got loud.
> 4. Scientists say it's mostly harmless gas: nitrogen, hydrogen, carbon dioxide, and a little methane.
> 5. But the world wanted to know: who started it?
> 6. Investigators checked the security footage. Enhance. Enhance.
> 7. It came from one elevator. One guy.
> 8. He blamed grandma. Grandma blamed the businessman.
> 9. But the truth? It started with one tiny pfft.

## Review as a hostile screenwriter / bored scroller
| Question | Verdict on v1 | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Opening funny or intriguing? | "What if…" is the generic Shorts opener, and the event only happens in line 2 (~3 s in). | Show the event at **frame 0** and *state* it: "Eight billion people just farted. At the exact same second." Then the number, the verb, the absurdity. |
| Premise instantly understandable? | Only after line 2. With sound off: unclear. | Big on‑screen title at frame 0 + a visual of everyone jolting with gas puffs. |
| First joke strong enough? | There is no joke until line 3. | Frame 0 *is* the joke: a silent, packed elevator plus a chorus of 5 different farts, then everyone's slow side‑eye. |
| Boring moments? | Line 4 is a chemistry list: pure exposition, ~4 s of nothing funny. It also names gases we could not source. | Replace with a set‑up/punchline: "Scientists said: relax, farting is normal. The smell is just a tiny bit of sulfur gas." *(beat)* "Tiny." Guests faint like dominoes. That is sourced (NHS, MedlinePlus) **and** it's a joke built on understatement. |
| Escalation entertaining? | "Investigators checked the footage" is told, not shown. | A CCTV "ENHANCE ×3" parody shown on screen with beeps and pixel zooms: visual comedy that needs no English. |
| Better twist? | The mascot being "patient zero" is fine but lands flat. | Add the **blame chain** that comes full circle: the kid blames *him*. Then add the contagious‑fart line as a playful fiction marker: "Farts aren't contagious… but this one?" |
| Memorable ending? | "It started with one tiny pfft" is told, so the joke is spoiled by the narrator. | The narrator **doesn't finish the sentence**: "It all started… with one tiny…" and the *sound* finishes it (*pfft*), which is also the first frame of the loop. |
| Does the loop work? | No. It ends on a statement. | Rewind to 3:13:59, the same elevator camera as frame 0, muzak continuing. The tiny pfft is literally the cause of the opening event, and the audio is continuous across the wrap. |
| Mascot involved throughout? | Absent in lines 2–5 (~10 s). | Mascot reaction inserts in every montage beat: shirt over nose, eyes watering, whistling innocently. |
| Removable scenes? | The library (line 3) duplicates the elevator joke ("quiet place"). | Cut the library and keep elevator, live TV and wedding (three different social stakes). |
| Funnier without confusion? | Too many places. | Three places + one planet shot. Each place gets one clear gag. |
| Length | ~40 s (the limit). | v2 ≈ 34 s. |

## Engagement problems in v1
None were integrated. v2 puts them **inside** jokes, spaced out, and never in the loop zone (see `05_engagement_plan.md`).
