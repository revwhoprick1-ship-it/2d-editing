# VIDEO_002 — Three Story Concepts & Comparison

## Concept A — "Patient Zero" (investigation + circular loop)
* **Hook:** frame 0, a packed silent elevator. Everyone jolts at once (gas puffs, a chorus of different farts). *"That was 8 billion people farting. At the same time."*
* **Chaos:** rapid montage: the elevator, a news anchor live on air, a wedding kiss, then the planet lighting up with puffs.
* **Escalation:** the science beat ("mostly odorless… the smell is a tiny bit of sulfur" → guests faint), then the whole world asks: *who went first?*
* **Twist:** CCTV "Enhance. Enhance. ENHANCE." lands on our mascot. A blame circle follows (he blames grandma → grandma blames the businessman → … → back to him).
* **Payoff / loop:** VHS rewind to 3:13:59. The quiet elevator, muzak, an innocent face… *"It all started with one tiny…"* *pfft* → **the video restarts at the moment everyone farts.** The ending is literally the cause of the opening.

## Concept B — "The Only One Who Didn't"
* **Hook:** the whole planet farts at once. Everyone except the mascot, who is holding it in.
* **Escalation:** the world realises he's the only one. Elevator passengers stare, the news shows his face, the pressure builds (sweat, puffed cheeks, a shaking camera).
* **Twist/climax:** he can't hold it. One mega fart launches the elevator up the shaft.
* **Loop:** his mega fart "triggers" the next global fart (back to the start).

## Concept C — "Planet Forecast"
* **Hook:** a news weather map: *"Today's forecast: 100% chance of farts."*
* **Escalation:** a "gas front" moves across continents; the mascot is a field reporter in the storm.
* **Twist:** the storm is coming from the studio itself.
* **Loop:** the forecast restarts tomorrow.

## Comparison (1–5)
| Criterion | A Patient Zero | B Only One | C Forecast |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hook strength (frame 0 + first line) | **5**: sound + visual + number | 5 (same opening) | 3: a weather map is slower to read |
| Comedy potential | **5**: many *different* jokes (awkward silence, understatement, parody, blame chain, innocent face) | 4: strong physical comedy but **one** joke stretched | 3: parody only |
| Visual opportunities | **5**: elevator, news, wedding, globe, CCTV, rewind | 4 | 3 |
| Escalation | **5**: local → global → investigation → accusation → reveal | 4 | 3 |
| Unexpected twist | **4**: the hero did it; the blame chain misdirects first | 4 | 2 |
| Payoff | **5**: reveal + callback | 4 | 2 |
| Loop potential | **5**: true cause→effect loop with the same camera and continuous audio | 4: needs "his fart triggers everyone" logic | 2 |
| Production cost (CPU only) | **4**: few small sets; most effects done in 2D editing | 3: explosion/launch VFX | 5 |
| **Total** | **38** | 32 | 23 |

**Decision: Concept A, with one stolen element from B.** The slow "everyone turns to look at him" stare becomes the reaction to the CCTV reveal.
