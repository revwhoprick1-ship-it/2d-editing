# VIDEO_002 — Engagement plan (like · comment · subscribe · share)

Rule: every prompt is **a joke inside the story**, appears on a beat where the viewer is already
laughing or judging, stays on screen long enough to read (≥ 1.6 s), and never overlaps another
prompt. Nothing appears in the **loop zone** (29.8 s → end → 0–4 s), so the replay is clean.

| Time (s) | Objective | On screen | Why it lands here |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4.1 – 6.3 | **SHARE** | `📤 SEND THIS TO YOUR ELEVATOR BUDDY` pill above the mascot pinching his nose | "In elevators" is the most relatable place in the video. Everybody has a person they'd send this to, and the prompt names that person instead of saying "share". |
| 14.7 – 18.0 | **SUBSCRIBE** | News ticker: `SUBSCRIBERS OF THIS CHANNEL REPORT ZERO SMELL: "WE SAW IT COMING" • ELEVATORS EVACUATED WORLDWIDE • WEDDING KISSES POSTPONED` | Fake breaking news is already on screen. Subscribing becomes a news headline: subscribers were warned. There's no voice‑over ask and no pause in the story. |
| 22.6 – 24.3 | **LIKE** | `👍 LIKE = GUILTY` chip pops onto the freeze frame of the mascot stamped `SUSPECT #1` | The viewer is judging him right now. A like becomes a vote in the trial. It leaves on the cut to the second accusation. |
| 24.3 – 29.2 | **COMMENT** | `💬 WHO DID IT? COMMENT 1, 2 OR 3` + numbered tags `1 GRANDMA`, `2 BUSINESSMAN`, `3 KID` following the blame chain | Comment bait with a built‑in argument: the chain closes back on the mascot, and the loop reveals him as Patient Zero. So the "correct" answer isn't 1, 2 or 3, and people correct each other in the comments ("it's 4!"). |

**Spacing:** 4.1 → 14.7 → 22.6 → 24.3 s. LIKE and COMMENT are adjacent but don't overlap (the
LIKE chip leaves at the cut on 24.33 s, and the COMMENT banner starts on that cut).

**Rejected ideas (and why):**
* An end‑card "subscribe for more". It would break the loop and is generic.
* The narrator saying "like and subscribe". It costs ~2 s of narration and interrupts the story.
* A pinned‑comment answer in the video. It would spoil the comment debate.

**Pinned comment to post at upload:** "Wrong answers only: who REALLY started it? 👀"
(This keeps the comment loop going without revealing the twist.)
