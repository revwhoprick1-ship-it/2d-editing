## Change log v1 → v2

* **Exposition:** the chemistry list became a sourced set‑up/punchline ("tiny bit of sulfur… Tiny." + domino faint).

* **Twist:** a blame chain that closes on the hero + an explicit fiction‑marker joke ("aren't contagious… but this one?").
* **Ending:** an unfinished sentence completed by the SFX, which is also the loop seam.
* **Removed:** the library scene (a duplicate joke) and the gas list (unsourced, not funny).
* **Added:** mascot reaction inserts in every montage beat.
* **Length:** ~40 s → ~34 s.
